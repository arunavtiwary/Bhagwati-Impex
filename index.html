About Us

Built on trust, driven by quality

In a Nutshell

Bhagwati Impex is a leading sawmill and wooden packaging manufacturer. We specialize in producing high-quality wooden pallets, crates, and boxes for industrial and export packaging requirements.

Backed by a team of skilled professionals, we manage the entire process — from sourcing quality timber to delivering finished products. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction sets us apart in the industry.

"Our main objective is to deliver excellent value to our customers by providing reliable, high-quality wooden packaging solutions with personalized service."
